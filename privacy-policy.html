Privacy Policy

Time Tracker Chrome Extension

Home > Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 7, 2026

This Chrome extension ("Time Tracker") does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers.

Authentication is performed using Google OAuth solely to allow the user to access their own Google Calendar. All data remains on the user's device and is used exclusively to provide the core functionality of the extension.

No information is shared with third parties and no data is used for tracking, advertising, or analytics purposes.

The developer does not store or process personal information outside the local browser environment.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you may contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store.
