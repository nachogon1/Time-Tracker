Effective Date: January 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Time Tracker. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Chrome extension. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information We Collect

Time Tracker is designed with privacy in mind. We collect and store the following information:

  • Time tracking data: The extension stores information about the time you spend on different websites and tasks locally in your browser.
  • Settings and preferences: Your configuration settings and preferences are stored locally.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by Time Tracker is used solely for:

  • Providing time tracking functionality
  • Displaying your time usage statistics
  • Maintaining your preferences and settings

4. Data Storage

All data collected by Time Tracker is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. We do not transmit your data to any external servers or third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information with third parties. Your time tracking data remains private and under your control.

6. Third-Party Services

Time Tracker does not integrate with or share data with any third-party services or analytics platforms.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data security through your browser's security settings.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You can access all your data through the extension interface
  • Delete: You can delete your data at any time by clearing the extension's storage or uninstalling the extension
  • Export: You can export your data through the extension's export functionality (if available)

9. Children's Privacy

Time Tracker is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store.
